About TAZHNEX Solutions

TAZHNEX Solutions is a transportation coordination service based in Karachi. We help customers find and arrange suitable transport for home and office moves, goods, vehicles, machinery, and commercial cargo — connecting your requirements with the right independent operators for the job.

We currently focus on Karachi and nearby areas, with plans to expand to additional cities. Our role is to understand what you need moved, help identify suitable transportation, and coordinate the process from request to delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you own the trucks?

We are a transportation coordination service. We help arrange suitable transportation with independent operators based on your requirements, rather than operating as a fleet-owning trucking company.

Which areas do you serve?

We currently focus on Karachi and surrounding areas. We plan to add more cities and nationwide service over time.

How much will it cost?

Pricing depends on your specific requirement. Submit a quote request and our team will get in touch to discuss suitable options and pricing.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the Request a Quote form above, or contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp. Our team will reach out to confirm details.

Can I send photos of what needs to be moved?

Yes — the quote form allows you to upload up to 5 photos, which helps us recommend suitable transportation.
